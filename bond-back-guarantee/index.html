Our bond-back guarantee

2What's covered

This guarantee applies to end-of-lease (bond) cleans. If your property manager, agent or landlord says an area or item we cleaned doesn't meet the standard at the final inspection, we'll come back and re-clean it at no cost to you.

It covers everything that was part of your original quote, including any extras you booked with it, such as carpet steam cleaning. It covers issues reported to us within 72 hours of your clean being completed.
