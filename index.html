            How much does an end-of-lease clean cost?

            It depends on your home's size and condition, so we give you one clear quote before you book. Tell us about your property and we'll call you within 2 minutes.

            How does the bond-back guarantee work?

            If your property manager raises an issue with anything that was part of your clean, send us their report or email within 72 hours of the clean and we'll come back and re-clean those areas at no cost. Read the full bond-back guarantee terms.

            What's included in an end-of-lease clean?

            Every room, to our end-of-lease checklist: the kitchen, including the oven, cooktop, rangehood and microwave; bathrooms and toilets; bedrooms and living areas, including built-in wardrobes; the laundry; inside windows and tracks, doors, skirting boards and light fittings; and all floors. Moving furniture, grout and silicone repairs, and fans, vents and air-conditioner filters aren't included.

            Is carpet steam cleaning included?

            It's an extra you can add when you request your quote, along with balcony cleaning, outside windows and wall deep cleaning. Your quote confirms the price.

            Do I need to be home during the clean?

            No, as long as we can access the property. We'll confirm access when we call.
